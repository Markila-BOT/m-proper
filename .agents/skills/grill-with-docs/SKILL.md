---
name: grill-with-docs
description: A relentless interview to sharpen a plan or design that writes the glossary terms and ADRs it settles as it goes.
---

Load and follow two skills, in this order:

1. `grilling` - interview in rounds until the design tree frontier is empty.
2. `domain-modeling` - capture resolved terms in `glossary/` and durable decisions in `adr/` while the interview runs.

Do not split the interview from the writing. A term or decision that settles in a round is recorded before the next round starts, so the discussion leaves documents behind instead of memory.

Stop before changing application code. This skill sharpens a plan; it does not implement one.
